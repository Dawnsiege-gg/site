How it fits together

Players join through a Velocity proxy. Every Paper server runs the core plus the game plugins you choose, and all nodes share one MariaDB and one Redis. Each node gets its own id and group in config.yml.

Read this before buying

Not included

  • Models, textures and resource packs. The custom items reference model ids that you provide.
  • Third-party plugins: Vault, an economy plugin, NuVotifier, Tebex and a custom-items plugin.
  • Server data, accounts, domains and hosting. You replace the placeholder domains in the configs with your own.

Known limitations

  • In dawnsiege-packs, the limited packs ship with empty item and command blocks, and the rarities and drop weights are a proposal. You fill them in for your server.
  • The pack catalog expects custom items you create. No models are provided.

Privacy

  • The core's tracking records chat, commands, private messages, container use and player positions, so staff can investigate. IP addresses are stored only as hashes.
  • You can turn it off in config.yml. If you keep it, say so in your server rules or privacy policy.
